# Simulation Contract

`SimulationKernel.simulate(profile, from_timestamp, to_timestamp, balance, lifecycle, care, survival, growth)` is biological simulation v6. AWAKE pets deplete hunger/hydration/energy/hygiene; SLEEPING pets deplete hunger/hydration/hygiene while recovering energy to 100 over 8h. Mood remains unchanged. After newborn protection, hunger zero loses 2 health/hour and hydration zero loses 4 health/hour; rates combine. Phase 7 language is outside biological simulation: it receives no clock and does not alter simulation state or event identity.

Growth v1 uses `current simulation timestamp - born_at`: CHILD at 172800 seconds, ADOLESCENT at 604800, ADULT at 1814400. It evaluates absolute thresholds, so offline catch-up and chunking preserve all individual transitions. A pet grows while SLEEPING or CRITICAL if it remains ALIVE; survival limits the biological end time at `died_at`, with equal-time `pet_grew` ordered before CRITICAL and death. DEAD pets are frozen. Every growth record is a schema-v1 `DomainEvent` constructed through `DomainEvent.make`; its ID contains simulation semantics, runtime growth balance version, pet, transition and canonical timestamp. Simulation IDs include simulation, passive, care, survival and growth versions plus subject/from/to. Pure simulation does not rewrite persisted configuration governance; Application projects deterministic growth/CRITICAL/death events into memory without changing biological output.
