# Simulation Contract

`SimulationKernel.simulate(profile, from_timestamp, to_timestamp, balance, lifecycle, care, survival)` accepts explicit data. AWAKE pets deplete hunger/hydration/energy/hygiene; SLEEPING pets deplete hunger/hydration/hygiene while recovering energy to 100 over 8h. Mood remains unchanged. After newborn protection, hunger zero loses 2 health/hour and hydration zero loses 4 health/hour; rates combine. The kernel integrates semantic boundaries (protection, need zero, CRITICAL and death) rather than per-second loops, so results and timestamps are chunking-independent. DEAD pets are frozen. Deterministic simulation IDs include simulation, passive-balance, care-balance and survival-balance versions plus subject/from/to.
