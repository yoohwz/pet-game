# Product Contract

The pet is offline-first: the device is authoritative, with elapsed time reconciled on launch and no continuous background process. A new profile receives one 4-hour incubating egg. The egg may become READY offline but never creates a pet unattended; player-present hatching must complete a safe transaction. An egg is not a pet and has no `born_at`; the pet identity and birth time are created only on successful completion.
