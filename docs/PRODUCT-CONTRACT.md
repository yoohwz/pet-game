# Product Contract

The pet is offline-first: the device is authoritative, with elapsed time reconciled on launch and no continuous background process. A new profile receives one 4-hour incubating egg. The egg may become READY offline but never creates a pet unattended; player-present hatching must complete a safe transaction. An egg is not a pet and has no `born_at`; the pet identity and birth time are created only on successful completion.

Phase 4 adds deprivation survival. Newborns have 12 hours of protection from deprivation damage, while their needs still decay. After protection, hunger and/or hydration at zero damage health. A living CRITICAL pet can be rescued by restoring every active zero need; rescue stops further damage but does not restore health. Death can happen offline and is reconstructed at its deterministic actual `died_at`; it is permanent and no revive exists. Memorialization is explicit, preserves history (including legacy historical counts), and never occurs unattended. A new egg after a memorial is likewise an explicit action; later pets do not erase memorial history. Growth and memory remain out of scope.
