# Product Contract

The pet is offline-first: the device is authoritative, with elapsed time reconciled on launch and no continuous background process. A new profile receives one 4-hour incubating egg. The egg may become READY offline but never creates a pet unattended; player-present hatching must complete a safe transaction. An egg is not a pet and has no `born_at`; the pet identity and birth time are created only on successful completion.

Phase 4 adds deprivation survival. Newborns have 12 hours of protection from deprivation damage, while their needs still decay. After protection, hunger and/or hydration at zero damage health. A living CRITICAL pet can be rescued by restoring every active zero need; rescue stops further damage but does not restore health. Death can happen offline and is reconstructed at its deterministic actual `died_at`; it is permanent and no revive exists. Memorialization is explicit, preserves history (including legacy historical counts), and never occurs unattended. A new egg after a memorial is likewise an explicit action; later pets do not erase memorial history.

Phase 5 adds local, bounded per-pet memories and persistent relationship. Meaningful care can increase bond, trust and cumulative care experience subject to a 300-second action cooldown; relationship has no passive decay. CRITICAL, rescue and death are remembered, and death freezes relationship/memory. Memorials preserve them, while a later pet starts with its own empty relationship and memory. Memory does not yet produce language or control Growth.

Phase 6 adds real-time deterministic growth. A living pet progresses by absolute age from `born_at`: NEWBORN before 2 days, CHILD at 2 days, ADOLESCENT at 7 days, and ADULT at 21 days. It can grow offline, while sleeping or CRITICAL, but care-spam, relationship, care experience and personality do not affect its stage. Death freezes the final stage.

Phase 7 adds offline language understanding for an ALIVE/AWAKE pet. The player may type an English/Vietnamese message; local deterministic rules classify a bounded intent/topics/sentiment and a non-verbal reaction/memory cue. The pet does not generate dialogue. Accepted messages are remembered locally and may later yield a repeat/familiar-topic cue, but language has no effect on needs, survival, growth, relationship, personality, sleep, or lifecycle. DEAD and SLEEPING pets reject messages; later pets never inherit prior language memory.
