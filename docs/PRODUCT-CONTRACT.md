# Product Contract

The pet is offline-first: the device is authoritative, with elapsed time reconciled on launch and no continuous background process. A new profile receives one 4-hour incubating egg. The egg may become READY offline but never creates a pet unattended; player-present hatching must complete a safe transaction. An egg is not a pet and has no `born_at`; the pet identity and birth time are created only on successful completion. Phase 4 adds deprivation survival: hunger/hydration at zero can cause CRITICAL then permanent death, including offline. Memorialization and a replacement egg are always explicit player actions; there is no unattended memorial, new egg, revival, growth, or memory system.
