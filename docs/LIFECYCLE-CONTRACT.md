# Lifecycle Contract

Lifecycle: `NEW GAME → EGG → INCUBATING → READY → HATCHING → ALIVE → NEWBORN → CHILD → ADOLESCENT → ADULT → CRITICAL → DEAD → MEMORIAL → optional EGG`. CRITICAL is a survival condition, not a life or growth state.

`life_state` (`ALIVE`/`DEAD`) and `growth_stage` are separate. A profile has at most one active subject; active egg and pet cannot coexist. Egg state has no `born_at`. INCUBATING/READY have no reservation fields. HATCHING requires a reserved pet ID, seed and start timestamp. INCUBATING becomes READY at its persisted threshold without creating a pet. Completion builds and atomically saves a PET candidate before replacing memory; failed completion remains HATCHING. A backward wall clock clamps birth to the current simulation timeline and records an anomaly.

For an ALIVE pet, activity is independent of life/growth state: `AWAKE` has no sleep timestamp; `SLEEPING` has a persisted start timestamp. Survival is `STABLE` or `CRITICAL`; rescue clears CRITICAL only once both hunger and hydration are above zero, without health recovery. `growth_stage` is a separate monotonic biological dimension with dedicated versioned growth metadata. It advances only by absolute age, including while sleeping or CRITICAL, and is frozen permanently by death. CRITICAL remains visibly represented even while the pet is sleeping, although sleep still exposes Wake only. A dead pet is memorialized only by an explicit candidate transaction; a replacement egg is likewise explicit and preserves all memorials.
